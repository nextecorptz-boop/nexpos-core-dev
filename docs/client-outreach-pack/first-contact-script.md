# NEXPOS: First-Contact Field Script

**Context:** Walking into a retail shop unannounced. 
**Goal:** Do not sell the software immediately. Sell a 5-minute meeting for later.
**Tone:** Respectful, brief, professional.

---

*(Approach the counter during a non-busy moment. Ask for the manager or owner.)*

**You:** "Habari. Samahani kwa usumbufu. Jina langu ni [Your Name], natoka Nextec Corp." *(Hello. Sorry to bother you. My name is [Your Name] from Nextec Corp.)*

**You:** "I noticed you handle a lot of inventory and foot traffic here. We build a modern checkout and inventory system called NEXPOS, specifically designed to stop stock discrepancies and speed up sales for shops like yours."

**You:** "I know you are busy running the shop right now. I’m not here to take up your time today. I just wanted to leave my card and ask if you'd be open to a quick 5-minute demo on my tablet sometime next week? It shows exactly how you can track your stock across branches directly from your phone."

*(If they say YES or are curious)*
**You:** "Great. What day is usually the quietest for you? I can bring the demo right to this counter. Also, what is the best WhatsApp number to send a quick reminder to?"

*(If they say NO or are too busy)*
**You:** "No problem at all. Please keep my card. If you ever feel like you're losing track of inventory or want a faster checkout system, we are here in Dar es Salaam and ready to help. Have a great sales day."
