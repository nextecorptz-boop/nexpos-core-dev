# NEXPOS: Pilot Offer Structure

**Objective:** Lower the barrier to entry by offering a controlled, low-risk test environment for the client.

## The Offer: 30-Day "Starter Pilot"

**What It Is:**
A 30-day deployment of the NEXPOS Early Access system in ONE of the client's busiest branches to prove stability, speed, and inventory accuracy.

**What is Included:**
- Initial creation of the secure cloud tenant environment.
- Import of up to 500 product SKUs/variants (provided by client via CSV).
- Creation of 3 user accounts (1 Owner, 1 Manager, 1 Cashier).
- 1 hour of on-site staff training.
- 30 days of standard technical support via WhatsApp during business hours.

**What is NOT Included (During Pilot):**
- Hardware (tablets, printers, barcode scanners must be provided by the client).
- Complex custom API integrations or custom reporting development.
- Multi-branch rollout (restricted to 1 branch to ensure a focused test).
- Immediate access to upcoming modules (Transfers, Expenses) until they are officially released.

**The Cost:**
- **Setup Fee:** TZS 150,000 (Discounted from TZS 350,000 for early adopters).
- **First 30 Days Subscription:** Waived (Free).

**The Decision Point (Day 25):**
On day 25 of the pilot, we hold a review meeting to look at the sales reports and inventory accuracy. If the client is satisfied, they transition to the standard "Retail Core" monthly subscription (e.g., TZS 80,000/month). If not, we export their sales data to CSV for them, and close the tenant at no extra cost.
