# NEXPOS: Feature List

## 🟢 Available Now (V1 Early Access Demo)
This core foundation is built, tested, and ready for deployment.

- **Role-Based Access Control:** Secure logins with strict permissions for Owners, Managers, and Cashiers.
- **Point of Sale (POS) Terminal:** Lightning-fast checkout interface designed specifically for cashiers.
- **Cash Transaction Processing:** End-to-end processing for cash payments with instant receipt generation.
- **Real-Time Inventory Tracking:** Live stock deductions at the moment of sale.
- **Product & Variant Management:** Comprehensive cataloging supporting product families, sizes, colors, and pricing tiers.
- **Management Dashboard:** Real-time overview of daily sales, transaction counts, and stock alerts.
- **Customer Directory:** Basic customer profile management to track who is buying from your store.
- **Basic Reporting:** High-level overview of sales performance.

## 🟡 Available During Early Access Rollout
These features are currently in active development and will be released to early adopters as over-the-air updates.

- **Advanced Receipt Printing:** Integration with thermal printers for physical receipts.
- **Barcode Scanning Support:** Plug-and-play support for standard USB/Bluetooth barcode scanners.
- **Detailed Shift Management (Till):** Opening and closing floats, cashier shift reports, and cash reconciliation.
- **Returns & Refunds Processing:** Secure workflows for handling returned merchandise.

## 🔵 Planned Future Modules (The Roadmap)
These core modules represent the future of the NEXPOS ecosystem.

- **Multi-Branch Transfers:** Seamlessly move stock between different store locations.
- **Expense Management:** Track day-to-day operational expenses directly within the POS.
- **Purchasing & Supplier Management:** Automate reordering and track supplier accounts.
- **Integrated Payments:** Direct integrations with Mobile Money (M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa) and Card terminals.
- **Credit Sales Registry:** Securely track and manage customer debt and layaways.
- **Control Center & Security Logs:** Detailed audit trails for every action taken in the system.
- **Advanced Custom Reporting:** Deep-dive analytics, profit margins, and predictive inventory reporting.
