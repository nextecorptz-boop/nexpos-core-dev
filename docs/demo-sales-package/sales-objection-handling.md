# NEXPOS: Sales Objection Handling

### 1. "Is this already complete?"
**Response:** "The core engine is functional, tested, and ready for deployment. This includes secure logins, live inventory tracking, and the fast POS checkout. We call it 'Early Access' because we are actively building additional advanced modules—like multi-branch transfers and expense tracking—which you will receive as updates. The foundation you need to run your shop today is operational."

### 2. "Can it work for my shop?"
**Response:** "Yes. NEXPOS was built specifically for Tanzanian retail environments. Whether you sell shoes, electronics, or boutique clothing, the system handles product variations easily and is designed to be fast enough for high-traffic environments."

### 3. "Can it support multiple branches?"
**Response:** "Absolutely. The architecture is built for multi-tenancy. You can track inventory across different physical locations from one central dashboard. Moving stock seamlessly between these branches is one of the modules we are rolling out very soon."

### 4. "Can it work with mobile money?"
**Response:** "Currently, cash transactions are recorded end-to-end within the system. For mobile money, your cashiers will continue receiving payments on your merchant phone/till, and then quickly record the sale in NEXPOS as a completed transaction. Direct API integration for automatic M-Pesa/Tigo Pesa prompts is on our immediate roadmap."

### 5. "Can you add expenses/purchases?"
**Response:** "Yes, those modules are already architected into our system. You can see their placeholders on the dashboard. Because we built a solid foundation first, we are able to roll out these expansions reliably in the coming weeks without disrupting your daily sales."

### 6. "Why should I pay if it is still early access?"
**Response:** "You are paying for the value the core system delivers today: stopping stock leakage, securing staff access, and speeding up your checkout. By joining as an early adopter, you lock in a lower pilot rate, and you get direct input into how the upcoming modules are shaped to fit your specific business."

### 7. "Can I use it on my phone?"
**Response:** "NEXPOS is a web application optimized for larger screens. While it can load on a phone, for the best experience at a busy checkout counter, we highly recommend using a tablet, laptop, or desktop computer."

### 8. "What happens if the internet goes off?"
**Response:** "NEXPOS is a cloud-based system that requires an internet connection to keep your inventory perfectly synchronized across branches in real-time. We recommend a basic WiFi router or a 4G connection at the store for the best performance."

### 9. "How secure is it?"
**Response:** "Extremely secure. We use secure cloud infrastructure (Supabase) to isolate your data. Your cashiers cannot access the manager's dashboard, and no one but you has access to the executive reports."

### 10. "Can you train my staff?"
**Response:** "Yes. The system is designed to be very intuitive—a cashier can learn it in 5 minutes. However, we offer dedicated on-site training packages to ensure your entire team is comfortable and confident using the software."
