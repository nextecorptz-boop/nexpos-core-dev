# NEXPOS: Screenshot Capture Checklist for Sales Material

When capturing these screenshots, ensure the browser window is clean (no extra tabs), the screen is maximized, and you are using the provided demo data.

### 1. Landing Page / Login
- **View:** The main `/auth/login` screen.
- **Purpose:** To show the modern, secure entry point of the application.
- **Caption:** "Robust security and role-based access control from day one."

### 2. Manager Dashboard
- **View:** `/app/dashboard` logged in as `manager@nexpos.dev`.
- **Purpose:** To highlight real-time operational visibility.
- **Caption:** "Your daily command center: Real-time sales metrics and stock alerts at a glance."

### 3. Product Catalog
- **View:** `/app/products` showing the list of shoes.
- **Purpose:** To demonstrate clean data management.
- **Caption:** "Organize your catalog effortlessly, supporting complex variations like sizes and colors."

### 4. Inventory Matrix
- **View:** `/app/inventory` showing stock levels.
- **Purpose:** To prove multi-branch tracking capability.
- **Caption:** "The single source of truth for your stock. Prevent stockouts and track every item across all your branches."

### 5. Cashier POS Interface
- **View:** `/app/pos` logged in as `cashier@nexpos.dev`.
- **Purpose:** To show the clean, distraction-free selling environment.
- **Caption:** "A lightning-fast, distraction-free POS terminal built specifically for speed at the checkout counter."

### 6. POS Cart & Checkout
- **View:** The POS screen with an item in the cart and the "Pay" modal open.
- **Purpose:** To show the simplicity of completing a transaction.
- **Caption:** "Process transactions in seconds with an intuitive checkout flow."

### 7. Owner Reports
- **View:** `/app/reports` logged in as `owner@nexpos.dev`.
- **Purpose:** To demonstrate high-level business intelligence.
- **Caption:** "Aggregate data from all your branches into actionable, high-level reports."

### 8. The Roadmap (Coming Soon)
