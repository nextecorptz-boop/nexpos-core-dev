# NEXPOS: 3-Minute Quick Demo Script

**Target Audience:** Busy retail owners, quick field sales encounters.
**Demo Environment:** `https://nexpos-v1-demo.vercel.app`
**Credentials:** Have tabs pre-logged in if possible, or use `manager@nexpos.dev` and `cashier@nexpos.dev` (Password: `password123`).

---

## 1. The Hook (30 seconds)
"Most retail systems are either too complex, too slow, or break when the internet drops. We built NEXPOS specifically for Tanzanian retail. It’s a modern retail operating system that gives you total control over your inventory, secure staff access, and a checkout experience that is lightning-fast."

## 2. The Core Value: Dashboard & Inventory (1 minute)
- **Action:** Show the Manager Dashboard. Click quickly to **Inventory**.
- **Talking Point:** "As a manager, this is your command center. You instantly see daily sales and real-time stock levels. Every time a sale is made, stock is updated instantly across the system. No more guessing what's on the shelves."

## 3. The Proof: Fast POS Checkout (1 minute)
- **Action:** Switch to the Cashier POS view.
- **Talking Point:** "For your cashiers, the interface is stripped down to just what they need to sell quickly. Let’s do a live sale."
- **Action:** Add an item to the cart, select 'Pay', choose 'Cash', and complete the checkout.
- **Talking Point:** "That took less than 5 seconds. Fast checkouts mean happy customers and shorter queues."

## 4. The Future & Closing (30 seconds)
- **Action:** Point to the sidebar showing upcoming modules.
- **Talking Point:** "This is our Early Access release—the core engine is live and working beautifully. We are already building out expenses, multi-branch transfers, and mobile money integrations on top of this. Are you open to running a pilot of this core system in one of your branches?"
